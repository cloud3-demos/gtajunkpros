FAQ

Common questions

How do I get a quote?

Text a photo to (647) 771-8099 or use the form above, and we’ll reply with a price. Bigger cleanouts may need a quick look in person.

Do I need to move anything to the curb?

No, we carry everything out from wherever it is, including basements and upper floors.

Which areas do you cover?

Mississauga, Brampton, Oakville, Etobicoke, Toronto, Milton, Burlington and Caledon.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes, rental cleanouts between tenants are one of our main services.

What can’t you take?

Hazardous materials like paint thinners, propane tanks and chemicals need special disposal, so ask us and we’ll point you to the right drop-off.
